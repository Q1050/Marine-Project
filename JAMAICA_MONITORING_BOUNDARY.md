# Jamaica operational monitoring boundary

The production assignment geometry is derived from the Flanders Marine
Institute (VLIZ) Marine Regions *Maritime Boundaries Geodatabase: Maritime
Boundaries and Exclusive Economic Zones (200NM), version 12* (2023-10-25),
MRGID 8459. Source product DOI: https://doi.org/10.14284/632.

The controlled source subset was obtained from the Marine Regions WFS in
EPSG:4326. The Jamaica/Colombia joint-regime feature returned by the country
filter is deliberately excluded; only the distinct Jamaican EEZ record is
activated. The stored coordinates are longitude/latitude WGS84 and are not
simplified by this project.

Marine Regions v12 combines treaty-derived lines with calculated median lines,
connection lines, and 200-nautical-mile limits. Its EEZ product includes
archipelagic, internal, and territorial waters, which differs from the strict
UNCLOS meaning of EEZ. The dataset is therefore used only as a prototype
country-level operational monitoring-assignment boundary.

It must not be described as an official Jamaican legal boundary or used for
navigation, adjudication, or natural-resource exploration. Boundary geometry
is used for platform monitoring assignment and should not be interpreted as an
independent legal determination of maritime limits.

Legal/reference context was checked against Jamaica's Maritime Areas Act and
the UN DOALOS Jamaica state file, including deposited archipelagic basepoints
and maritime delimitation agreements with Cuba and Colombia. Those materials
are legal references; they are not represented as the direct source of the
stored operational GIS polygon.
