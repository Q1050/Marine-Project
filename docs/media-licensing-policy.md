# Identification-media licensing policy

Eligibility fails closed per asset:

- Public domain / CC0: eligible after scientific and quality review.
- Attribution-required (for example CC BY): eligible only with creator, source, license, and attribution preserved.
- Share-alike: separate policy review required; not currently corpus eligible.
- Non-commercial: reference-only.
- No-derivatives: excluded from model-development use.
- Unknown or prohibited: excluded.

Provider labels do not broaden rights. Original attribution and license references remain bound to metadata and exports.
