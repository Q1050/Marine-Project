# Governed identification corpus

Identification media is regional model-development support, not jurisdiction occurrence or ecological evidence. A corpus has a stable key/version, intended purpose, taxonomic scope, configuration, provenance, limitations, lifecycle, reviewer metadata, and deterministic fingerprint.

Assets retain governed taxon identity, provider identity, provider asset ID, immutable source reference, creator, license, attribution, content hash, controlled artifact reference, dimensions, acquisition date, supplied biological metadata, source event/specimen identity, quality state, duplicate state, and review result. Unknown life stage, sex, orientation, locality, or date remains null; it is never inferred.

Only explicitly reviewed assets with eligible licensing, valid quality, and unique duplicate state may enter a training export. Corpus availability never establishes presence in Jamaica or any other jurisdiction.
