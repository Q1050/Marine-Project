# Agency pilot walkthrough

1. Sign in with the pilot account supplied through a secure channel. Confirm the header shows the intended jurisdiction and role.
2. Open **Observation Review**. Filter to unassigned or your cases, claim a report, and inspect the submitted private image.
3. Compare reporter context and the AI suggestion. AI is preliminary. Confirm, correct, mark unresolved/rejected, handle duplicates, or request more information using the evidence available.
4. After expert verification, inspect scientific eligibility. Verification does not automatically create governed occurrence evidence.
5. If separately granted scientific-review authority, open **Early Warning**. Inspect the assessment, map, governed baseline, suitability and descriptive context.
6. Claim the assessment and record a governed disposition. “Review suggested” is not confirmed invasion.
7. Browse the jurisdiction species directory. Empty governed invasive results are valid when no approved authority exists.
8. Submit feedback from the reviewer home page with the relevant workflow/page context. Do not paste passwords, reporter tokens, or private evidence.

Demo environments are visibly marked and contain demonstration data only.
