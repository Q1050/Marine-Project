# Corpus splitting and leakage protection

Splits are deterministic and seeded. Assets sharing an underlying source event or specimen remain together. Where those identifiers are unavailable, perceptual duplicate group, exact SHA-256 group, then stable asset identity are used.

Groups—not individual images—are assigned to train, validation, or test. The split seed, ratios, group key, version, and inclusion fingerprint are retained. A final production split must not be created until governed coverage and diversity are scientifically reviewed. Split assignment does not make an asset eligible; licensing, quality, duplicate, and review gates still apply.
