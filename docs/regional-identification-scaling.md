# Regional identification scaling

Scaling is governed per taxon and does not assume equal media volume. Each candidate taxon needs an accepted authority identity, approved regional registry entry, provider availability, item-level reusable licensing and attribution, technical validation, duplicate/event grouping, and explicit human review.

Before a 100–300 taxon acquisition, estimate provider request volume, rate limits, bounded concurrency, retry burden, artifact storage, taxonomy-resolution workload, reviewer capacity, life-stage/view/context gaps, benchmark independence, and negative/open-set coverage. Batch acquisition may prepare candidates but cannot approve taxonomy or media.

The machine-readable preflight contract is in `regional-identification-scaling.json`.
