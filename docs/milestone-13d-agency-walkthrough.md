# Future agency early-warning walkthrough

Use a disposable, authorized fixture rather than production observations:

1. An operational reviewer confirms or corrects an observation identity.
2. The observation becomes eligible and an event is recorded; it is not automatically evaluated while automation is disabled.
3. An authorized scientific reviewer explicitly evaluates or opens the resulting assessment.
4. The reviewer inspects occurrence, distance, temporal, suitability, ecological context, and the governed-evidence map.
5. The reviewer claims and acknowledges, dismisses, escalates, or resolves the assessment with a concise reason.
6. The immutable history demonstrates who acted and when.

The reviewer may toggle the selected observation, marine boundary, governed baseline, verified non-duplicate observations, and environmental suitability. Suitability cells come only from the active internal scientific deployment and remain contextual.

An operational reviewer sees whether expert verification made the observation eligible, why it is blocked when prerequisites fail, and whether an assessment exists. A separately authorized scientific reviewer claims and dispositions the assessment.

Always state: escalation means further scientific or organizational attention. It does not mean emergency, confirmed invasion, or range expansion. Production automated anomaly detection is not active.
