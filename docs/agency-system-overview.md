# Caribbean Marine Monitoring Platform

Marine sightings and scientific evidence are often fragmented across public reports, datasets, and institutional workflows. This platform offers one controlled place to report, review, organize, map, and scientifically assess marine observations.

It can accept sightings, assist identification, support expert review, maintain governed jurisdiction species information, map historical/governed evidence, show environmental habitat suitability where reviewed, identify observations that may deserve scientific attention, and preserve audit/provenance.

It does **not** make official declarations through AI. An anomaly is not an invasion. Suitability is not presence or occurrence probability. Regional status is not copied to a country. A public report is not automatically scientific evidence. Operational priority is not ecological severity.

For a participating agency, the potential value is a traceable queue from incoming report through expert review and separate scientific attention, without collapsing those distinct authorities. Participation or organization metadata does not imply endorsement or grant scientific authority.
