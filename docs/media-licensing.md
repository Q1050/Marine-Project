# Media licensing firewall

Training permission and public-display permission are independent.

Conservative asset classifications include `TRAINING_ALLOWED`, `ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED`, `REFERENCE_ONLY`, `NONCOMMERCIAL_RESTRICTION`, `DERIVATIVES_RESTRICTED`, `LICENSE_UNRESOLVED`, and `EXCLUDED`. These record reviewed source semantics; they do not replace the source license text.

Training export currently permits only explicitly reviewed `TRAINING_ALLOWED` and `ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED` assets. Unresolved, reference-only, noncommercial, derivatives-restricted, excluded, corrupt, or duplicate assets fail closed. Creator, license expression, source reference, and attribution accompany every export item. Legal interpretation remains an organizational responsibility.
