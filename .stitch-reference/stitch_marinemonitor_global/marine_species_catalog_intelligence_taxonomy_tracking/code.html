Red Lionfish

Pterois volitans

Indo-Pacific predatory marine teleost established throughout the Greater Caribbean Basin, Gulf of Mexico, and Western Atlantic. Recognized driver of dramatic coral-reef juvenile teleost recruitment declines.

Supraocular Tentacles & Translucent Membranes

Apex Generalist Predator

Mesophotic Depth Refuge
